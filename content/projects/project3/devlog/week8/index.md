## Week 8 – Services and Categories

This week I focused on creating and organizing the different services offered by the **Maler & Håndværk** web application.

Painting is the main service, while the application also includes other home improvement and repair services.

### Service Categories

- Malerarbejde
- Elektrikerarbejde
- VVS-arbejde
- Tømrerarbejde
- Murerarbejde
- Reparationer i hjemmet

### Service Management

I worked on how services can be:

- Created
- Updated
- Activated or deactivated
- Connected to customer requests
- Organized into different categories

### What I Learned

- How to organize services into categories
- How services can be stored in the database
- How customers can select a service
- How services can be connected to other entities
