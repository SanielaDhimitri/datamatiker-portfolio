This week, I focused on the **frontend and user interface** of the Villa Room Booking System.

I worked on making the application easier to use and improving how information is presented to the user.

The goal was to create a clear interface where guests can view rooms and use the booking functionality, while the administrator can easily access the management features.

## What I worked on

- **Frontend structure** – Organized the pages and user interface of the application.
- **Room presentation** – Worked on how room information is displayed.
- **Booking interface** – Improved the interface used for creating bookings.
- **Navigation** – Worked on making it easier to move between the different parts of the system.
- **Administrator interface** – Organized the functionality available to the administrator.
- **User experience** – Improved the overall usability and presentation of the application.
