This week, I focused on **room management** in the Villa Room Booking System.

I worked on the functionality needed to manage the rooms available in the villa. The goal was to make sure that room information can be stored, displayed and managed correctly in the system.

I also worked on organizing the room data so that it can later be used by the booking functionality.

## What I worked on

- **Room entity** – Worked with the data and attributes needed to represent a room.
- **Room information** – Organized information such as room name, capacity, description and availability.
- **Room management** – Worked on functionality for managing rooms in the system.
- **Database integration** – Connected room information with the database.
- **Administrator functionality** – Worked on functionality that allows the administrator to manage room information.
