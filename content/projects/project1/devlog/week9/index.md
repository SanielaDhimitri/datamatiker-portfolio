This week, I focused on implementing the **booking functionality** for the Villa Room Booking System.

I worked on the process that allows guests to select a room and create a booking.

The booking functionality connects the guest, the selected room and the booking dates so that the necessary information can be stored and managed in the system.

## What I worked on

- **Booking process** – Worked on the process for creating a new booking.
- **Booking data** – Organized the information needed for a booking.
- **Room selection** – Connected bookings with the selected room.
- **Booking dates** – Worked with check-in and check-out dates.
- **Database integration** – Stored booking information in the database.
- **Booking overview** – Worked on displaying and managing existing bookings.
