This week, I focused on **booking validation and room availability** in the Villa Room Booking System.

I worked on making sure that a room can only be booked when it is available for the selected dates.

I also worked on validating booking information before saving it to the database. This helps prevent invalid bookings and overlapping reservations.

## What I worked on

- **Room availability** – Checked whether a room is available for the selected period.
- **Date validation** – Validated check-in and check-out dates.
- **Booking overlap** – Worked on preventing overlapping bookings.
- **Input validation** – Checked booking information before saving it.
- **Error handling** – Worked on handling invalid booking attempts.
- **Booking rules** – Added business rules related to the booking process.
