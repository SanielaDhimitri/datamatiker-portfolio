This week, I focused on **integrating and testing** the different parts of the Villa Room Booking System.

I connected the main components of the application and checked that they work correctly together. This included the database, business logic, booking functionality and user interface.

I also tested important workflows to identify problems and make sure that the system behaves as expected.

## What I worked on

- **System integration** – Connected the different parts of the application.
- **Database integration** – Tested communication between the application and the database.
- **Booking flow** – Tested the complete process of creating and managing a booking.
- **Functionality testing** – Checked that the main features work correctly.
- **Validation testing** – Tested validation rules and invalid input.
- **Error handling** – Checked how the application handles errors and unexpected situations.
